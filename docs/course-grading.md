# Course grading

The students evaluation and grading is composed by two components:

* Final individual written exam, done on the regular exam dates. 
    * 30% of the final course grade.
    * A minimum grade of 9.5 is required.

* Exercise sets and final project, done during the first 15 weeks.
    * 70% of the final course grade.
    * Divided into three _parts_: two exercise sets and a final project.
    * Each part will have a discussion and a per-student grade.
    * minimum grade of 8.0 is required for each part.
    * A minimum grade of 9.5 is required for this practical component.
    * Done by groups of three students.
